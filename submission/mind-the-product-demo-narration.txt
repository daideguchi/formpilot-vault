FormPilot Vault is for solo builders and small teams who fill out the same web forms again and again.

Every product launch creates more signup forms, lead forms, trial forms, event forms, and partner forms.
Browser autofill helps with simple pages, but it often fails when forms split names, ask for phonetic names, divide addresses, or use site-specific business fields.

FormPilot turns that repeated work into a reviewable fill plan.
First, the Chrome extension reads the form structure.
Then it maps each field to a safe profile key.
The user reviews the plan.
Only after that review does FormPilot fill the page.
It never presses submit.

The privacy boundary is the product.
AI can help understand the form, but raw personal values stay in the local Vault.
Names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, passwords, cookies, and selectors are not sent to AI.
If live AI is unavailable, FormPilot still works with deterministic rules fallback.

The demo shows the public landing page, the extension popup, a real browser fill, public form probes, and the upgrade path.
The proof is in the repository: real browser tests fill twelve fields, public probes run without submitting forms, and Novus Pendo is installed with dashboard proof.

The idea is simple.
People are shipping more with AI, but the boring operational work around shipping is still real.
FormPilot Vault helps them move faster without giving up human review or privacy.
