Terms

AI Form Autofill is a user-controlled form filling assistant. Users are responsible for reviewing all filled values before submitting any form.

Permitted Use

Use the extension to fill forms that you opened yourself and are allowed to complete. The extension is designed for signups, inquiries, event registrations, trial registrations, and similar everyday forms.

Prohibited Use

  • Do not use it for bulk account creation.
  • Do not use it to bypass CAPTCHA, identity checks, SMS verification, or email verification.
  • Do not use it on forms where automation is forbidden by the website's terms.
  • Do not rely on it for regulated financial, medical, legal, government, or identity verification workflows without separate review.

Plans

Free usage is limited to 5 fills per month. Paid plans unlock higher or unlimited usage and additional profile or team features according to the current pricing page.
